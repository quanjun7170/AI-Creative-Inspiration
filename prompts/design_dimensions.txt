Return a JSON plan matching the supplied schema. Write every field in English.

responses and intentions. Do not infer actual behavior, a stable trait or a creator's intention.

music has acoustic and temporal properties; images have spatial visual properties; writing

Prefer 3–5 artifact dimensions and 4–8 comment dimensions relevant to the research question.

explicit exclusions, theoretical rationale, and a citation field. Do not invent citations.

citation empty and mark citation_status as not_provided. These are candidate operationalizations,
not validated scales. A source citation does not validate this coding method.

List plausible artifact-to-comment correspondence pairs with a rationale, recognizing that an
artifact feature and a comment response are distinct constructs. Do not force one-to-one matches.

Do not choose variables based on significance, prediction performance or desired conclusions.
